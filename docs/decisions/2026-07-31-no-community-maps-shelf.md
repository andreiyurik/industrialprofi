# No open community-maps shelf

An open-publishing shelf with ratings (roadmap.sh/community style) fills with shallow
duplicates, breaks the "everything here is curated" promise, adds a moderation surface,
and star ratings farm like a leaderboard. The catalog stays single and curated.

The agreed future shape is **sandbox + promotion**: a member builds a draft map only
they see (zero moderation) with one button, «предложить в каталог», feeding the existing
`pending_review` pipeline. The sandbox half shipped as
[personal maps](../architecture/personal-maps.md); promotion is not built.
