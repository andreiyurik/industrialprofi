# The hero names the contributor door

The first screen used to speak to the learner only: «Рабочая профессия — по настоящим
нормативам», one button, and every contributor door below the catalog. Now the heading
is «Профессии, на которых всё держится», and one line says who writes the maps — with
a red-pencil correction, «методисты» struck out for «практики». The two audiences are
the two actions: the button «Учусь — выбрать профессию» and a quiet link «Работаю —
поправить карту» to `/contribute`. The drawing's title block credits the work
(«Разраб. сообщество», «Пров. практики»), leaves the standards check vacant
(«Н. контр. — это ты?»), counts accepted revisions with a link to `/changes` and states
the price, «Цена 0 ₽». The hero is three things — heading, one line, actions — with
red kept for the pencil alone; the «project is young, write to me» banner moved from
above the header to above the footer on every page.

**Why.** The heading read like a training centre's, and nothing on the first screen
said that the maps are written and corrected by people. «Рабочих» pulled a
vocational-school tone and was inaccurate for an АСУ ТП engineer; «полезных» and
«прикладных» were weighed and dropped — respect comes from what the trades hold up, not
from an adjective. A specialist is invited to check and correct, as a senior, rather
than to «help the project». «Рабочие» stays in the page title and description, where
search needs it.

Kept out on purpose: counters of people and avatars (they would show an empty room), a
deliberate mistake on the drawing (it costs trust on a site about standards), striking
out the word «рабочие» itself, and a second toy next to the pump. No new mechanic: copy,
one CSS animation and the title block, so the
[users-before-features](2026-09-21-users-before-features.md) pause holds.

This narrows [community doors](2026-10-13-community-doors.md): the door is named on the
first screen, but the work queues — illustrations, the wanted list — still live below
the learner's path.
