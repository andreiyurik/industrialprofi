# No wiki social governance

No arbitration, RfA-style voting, granular permission tiers or checkuser. At this scale
they are «лишние механики». We keep Wikipedia's *data* mechanics — immutable revisions,
the append-only admin log — and the simple `member → editor → administrator` ladder.
