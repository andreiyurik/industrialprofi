# Community doors

The project had no authors and no author applications. The only door was «вести
профессию»: a three-field application for a role, which is too big a first step, and the
illustration gap was visible only to editors. A banner alone would not fix that — it is
seen only by people who already came.

- **Every gap is public.** `/illustrations` lists each pending brief across published
  professions; `/changes` shows every accepted revision with its author. An empty room
  repels; a visible queue invites.
- **The smallest unit of help is one link.** A reader suggests an illustration from the
  placeholder itself or from the queue; it folds into a `Feedback` on the lesson, and an
  editor or the founder fills the slot. No member upload, no new model, no migration — the
  [editor-gated images decision](2026-06-27-lesson-images-editor-gated.md) stands.
- **Attribution, not competition.** Recent changes is a chronological feed, never a
  ranking ([no leaderboard](2026-06-24-no-leaderboard.md)).
- **Outside the app is where the people are.** Trade chats, an external Telegram group and
  Wikimedia Commons are the plan in [COMMUNITY](../COMMUNITY.md); this decision only
  removes the friction they would hit on arrival.

Within the [users-before-features](2026-09-21-users-before-features.md) carve-out:
illustrations for the live professions come before anything new. Not done on purpose: a
redesign (the serious look suits the trade; the empty room was the problem), a member
file upload, an in-app chat.
