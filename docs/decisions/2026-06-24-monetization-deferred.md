# Monetization deferred

Materials stay free and open forever; money, if any, is charged for outcomes and
services around the content, never for entry. Retention comes before revenue. The v0.4
certificate plan is deferred (design kept in [VISION](../VISION.md#deferred-design--verified-certificates-was-the-v04-plan)).
The most promising path is B2B — training centres and employers.
