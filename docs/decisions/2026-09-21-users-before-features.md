# Users before features

The roadmap lists about thirty shipped mechanics, built by one person, with no
measured usage behind them. The thirtieth feature adds less than the tenth learner.

- **Pause** new mechanics, new professions and refactors for their own sake. Content
  fixes and bugs still ship.
- **Measure one number:** learners who come back in the second week and mark a lesson
  done. The admin dashboard and `WeeklyCounts` already hold the raw counts.
- **One profession first** — the electrician path is the deepest — and real
  electricians from Telegram channels, training centres and colleges walking through it.
- **Resume features only from where they get stuck.** Illustrations, tasks and links
  for that profession come before anything new.

Personal maps and web-filled illustrations shipped just before this call; they stay
and are not extended until usage shows they matter.
